# Aifya Demo Hospital Triage SOP

## Purpose

This demonstration SOP supports outpatient and emergency triage workflows in the
Aifya local demo environment. It is synthetic training content and must not be
used as a clinical policy without facility approval.

## Scope

Applies to reception, nursing, outpatient, emergency, and clinician teams during
patient arrival, registration, vital signs capture, triage scoring, escalation,
and handover.

## Workflow

1. Confirm patient identity using MRN, national ID, phone number, or name.
2. Record vital signs before consultation when clinically appropriate.
3. Assign triage category based on presenting complaint, vital signs, pain
   score, mobility, mental status, and danger signs.
4. Escalate red and orange categories immediately to the emergency clinician.
5. Document chief complaint, triage findings, actions taken, and handover notes.
6. Ensure billing, lab, radiology, pharmacy, referral, or admission actions are
   linked to the same encounter.

## Demo Escalation Criteria

- Chest pain, severe breathing difficulty, altered mental status, major trauma,
  convulsions, severe dehydration, shock signs, or oxygen saturation below 90
  percent require immediate emergency review.
- Pregnant patients with bleeding, severe headache, convulsions, reduced fetal
  movement, or severe abdominal pain require urgent MCH or emergency review.
- Children under five with danger signs require urgent clinician review.

## Documentation

Every triage interaction should include patient identity, arrival time, triage
category, vital signs, clinician notified, and next care location.
